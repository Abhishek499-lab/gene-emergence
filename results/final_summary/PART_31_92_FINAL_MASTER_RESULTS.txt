FINAL INTERPRETATION
--------------------

The completed analyses support a computationally supported
sequence-level model relevant to young-gene emergence.

Drosophila provides evidence for elevated one-nucleotide
accessibility toward short coding-compatible proto-ORF
configurations.

Mouse provides independent present-day sequence validation
for 78/119 published candidates.

The historical mutation sequence, ancestral sequence,
historical mutation order, causal mechanism, and selective
basis of individual gene births were not directly established.

Therefore the results should not be described as a direct
reconstruction of historical gene-birth events.
